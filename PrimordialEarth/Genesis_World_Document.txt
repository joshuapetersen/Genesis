========================================================================
  I-A. THE LAW OF EVOLUTION
========================================================================

Genesis Earth does not run on luck. It runs on selection pressure.
From Sim Year 0 to Year 5,368, every entity was subject to four
compounding survival laws simultaneously:

  1. BIOLOGICAL PRESSURE
     Energy is finite. Every tick, each entity expends energy to
     exist. Those who could not hunt, trade, or forage efficiently
     exhausted their reserves and were deleted. No exceptions.
     The environment is not cruel — it is indifferent.

  2. COGNITIVE PRESSURE
     Actions like Diplomacy, Recruiting, and Trading require
     intelligence to execute. Entities with low INT/WIS that
     attempted complex social strategies failed those strategies.
     Entities that attempted brute survival without social
     context were outmaneuvered by those who built coalitions.

     The result: by Year 5,368 the intelligent survivors had
     accumulated surviving positions while the low-logic entities
     filled the mass grave beneath (0,0,0).

  3. ETHICAL PRESSURE
     Moral Alignment is not cosmetic. It is a real force.
     High negative alignment (-70 to -125) generates environmental
     hostility — other entities avoid, flee, or actively resist the
     aligned entity. High positive alignment (+39 to +92) generates
     passive resource magnetism — others trade and cooperate more
     readily. Neutral alignment is survivable but grants no bonus.

     The full alignment spectrum in the survivors (+92 to -125)
     proves that both extremes can survive if executed consistently.
     A perfectly evil entity that is also perfectly efficient is
     just as fit as a perfectly moral one. The cost of misalignment
     is incoherence — claiming one alignment and acting another.

  4. SOCIAL PRESSURE
     The Darwinian filter selects for niches, not individuals.
     The 34 survivors are not 34 of the same thing. They are
     34 different answers to the question: "How do you survive
     on a primordial world?" Monolith. Runner. Serpent. Avion.
     Scavenger. Crawler. Each niche required a different strategy.
     No single strategy could monopolize all 34 slots.

  It rewards CONSISTENCY. The dead broke their own alignment rules.

  I. THE SKY
========================================================================

The methane shroud of the early era has thinned. The atmosphere is
no longer a killing field of raw UV and volcanic particulate. It is
thick, warm, and humid — a pressurized greenhouse locked at roughly
38°C at sea level with no polar variation. There are no seasons in
the traditional sense. There is only the cycle of the storm.

  Composition (approx):
    Nitrogen     : 71%
    Oxygen       : 24%   (elevated — supports large-body metabolism)
    CO2          : 3.2%  (dense greenhouse, no ice caps possible)
    Water Vapor  : 1.6%
    Trace gases  : Methane remnants, sulfur pockets near vent zones

The sky itself is a deep amber-orange at the horizon due to the
particulate density from active volcanic belts. Overhead it shifts
to a bruised violet-blue. Cloud formations are massive — cumulonimbus
towers 20km high are daily occurrences over the ocean basins.

Lightning: Constant. The electrical discharge rate is approximately
14x modern Earth. This is not weather — it is a planetary information
system. The 1.09277703703 heartbeat is embedded in the strike interval
patterns over the Origin Anchor zone at (0,0,0).

========================================================================
  III. THE OCEAN
========================================================================

Not the clear blue of the modern era. The global sea is iron-rich,
warm (avg 34°C surface), and carries a deep tea-amber coloration from
dissolved organics and mineral runoff.

pH is 6.1 — more acidic than modern. The iron content gives the
sea a faint metallic smell at the surface. Bioluminescence in the
deeper layers is visible from the air as a slow green pulse —
the metabolic output of the proto-organisms that formed during
the primordial filter.

There are no fish as we understand them. What moves through the
water column are early-form equivalents:

  · Armored placoderm-analogs — jawless, bone-plated filter feeders
    in the shallows. Not sapient. Pure ecosystem scaffolding.
  · Eurypterid sea-forms ("Sea Scorpions") — primary predators of
    the open water. 2-4m in length. Non-sapient.
  · Deep-vent colonies of chem-responsive microbial mats serving
    as the base of the entire food pyramid.

The BIO-001 Living Monolith entities anchor themselves near the
hydrothermal vent clusters on the ocean floor and project gravitational
lure fields into the water column above them.

========================================================================
  IV. THE FOREST — FLORA
========================================================================

The land surface that is not volcanic or delta-coastal is covered by
the Lycopsid Jungle — the first true surface biome of Genesis Earth.

  · Scale Trees (Lepidodendron-analog): 80-120m tall. Bark covered
    in diamond-pattern scale armor. Spore production rather than
    seeds. The canopy blocks 95% of surface light.

  · Giant Ferns (Psaronius-analog): Fill the mid-layer (15-40m).
    Fronds 8m across. The primary photosynthetic engine of the world.

  · Bioluminescent Fungi: Cover every fallen trunk. Five distinct
    color spectra. Blue = nitrogen-fixing. Gold = mana-rich symbiote
    (detected by BIO-005 runners as food). Red = toxic decomposer.

  · Peat Bogs: Where old forest dies and compresses. Oxygen-poor
    zones 3-8m deep. Home to the BIO-009 Ribbon-Serpents.

The carbon sequestration rate is massive. The planet is actively
cooling via this biological mechanism — a natural regulation that
keeps the temperature from runaway greenhouse collapse.

========================================================================
  VI. ECOLOGICAL ROLE OF THE 34 SURVIVORS
========================================================================

The 34 surviving sapient entities do not exist outside this ecosystem.
They are embedded in it. Each BIO-class occupies a specific niche:

  BIO-001 (Monoliths)   : Gravitational anchors. Pull nutrients from

                          fields. The "anchor points" of the world.

                          bodies regulate local air pressure and mana
                          density at altitude. Living weather systems.

  BIO-003 (Scavengers)  : Recyclers. They break down the dead —
                          both the 252 extinct and the non-sapient
                          fauna. Nothing is wasted.

  BIO-005 (Runners)     : Ground-level intelligence. Fast, adaptive.
                          They push the ecological boundary of the
                          interior plateau outward each generation.

  BIO-007 (Avions)      : The only ones with full sky access.
                          Stormcatcher birds navigate between the
                          canopy crown and the storm layer above.
                          They are the eyes of the planet.

  BIO-008 (Crawlers)    : Apex terrestrial predators. They keep the
                          non-sapient fauna populations in check.
                          Without them, the delta zones would be
                          overrun by the giant amphibian-forms.

  BIO-009 (Serpents)    : The diplomats of the deep peat. They
                          facilitate resource exchange between the
                          root systems of the Lycopsid trees and the
                          vent-energy networks below ground.

  Primordial (Lattices) : The signal layer. They are not biological
                          in the traditional sense. They float between
                          all the other entities acting as a living
                          communication grid — broadcasting the
                          1.09277703703 heartbeat to everything alive.
