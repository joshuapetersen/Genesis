# 05 - The UBM Evolutionary Trees (Examples)

## Synthesis Mechanics
A UBM (Unique Boss Monster) is not born; it is forged by extreme circumstance. Normal entities follow linear paths (Beetle -> Big Beetle), but a true UBM requires the Tri-Synthesis: **Genome + Environment + Trauma**.

Here are 5 example UBM evolutionary trees defined for the logic engine:

### 1. The World-Tree Yggdrasil (Flora Apex)
- **Base Entity:** Flora_AncientOak
- **Genome Requirement:** [Immortality Clause]
- **Environment:** Arcane Pool (Mana Saturation > 10,000)
- **Trauma:** "Survived 500,000 Cycles"
- **Result:** Instead of dying of old age, it awakens. Scale multiplies by 100x. It ceases to be an entity and becomes an Environment, generating its own Arcane pool and spawning Lesser Ents to defend it.

### 2. The Arcane Terror-Carapace (Bug Apex)
- **Base Entity:** Insect_Scarab
- **Genome Requirement:** [Arcane Conduit]
- **Environment:** Arcane Pool
- **Trauma:** "Prey's Paranoia" (Survived lethal hit with < 5% HP from a Beast).
- **Result:** The scarab mutates into a massive, magic-casting tank. It abandons scavenging and actively hunts the specific Beast species that almost killed it. 

### 3. The Blood-Moon Fenrir (Beast Apex)
- **Base Entity:** Predator_DireWolf
- **Genome Requirement:** [Apex Predator]
- **Environment:** The Blood Plateau
- **Trauma:** "Pack Slaughter" (Was the sole survivor when the rest of its pack was killed by Sapients or Monsters).
- **Result:** Becomes a 30-foot tall direwolf that specifically hunts Sapients. It emits the [Pack Hunter] aura globally to ALL wolves, turning the entire forest into a massive hunting ground.

### 4. The Sovereign Warlord (Monster Apex)
- **Base Entity:** Monster_Goblin
- **Genome Requirement:** [Swarm Sovereign]
- **Environment:** Caverns/Mithril 
- **Trauma:** "Forging the Crown" (Must have successfully killed a Sapient and looted an iron/mithril weapon).
- **Result:** Goblins are normally dumb. This one gains human-level INT. It organizes the other 1,000 goblins into a structured military, builds actual fortresses, and begins a systemic war against the Sapient faction.

### 5. The Void-Walker (Glitch/Entity)
- **Base Entity:** ANY
- **Genome Requirement:** ANY
- **Environment:** The Void Rifts (Edge of the map bounds)
- **Trauma:** "Physics Clip" (Survived falling through the Z-axis due to a collision error).
- **Result:** The system cannot delete it. It mutates into a horrific, shape-shifting entity. Its Speed is infinite (it teleports). Its attacks bypass Armor and VIT entirely, dealing direct damage to the target's Soul File.
