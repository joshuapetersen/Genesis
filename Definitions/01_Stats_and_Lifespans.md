# 01 - Core Stats & Lifespans

## The 6 Pillars of Existence
Every entity in the Sovereign Simulation is bound by six cardinal attributes.

1. **Vitality (VIT)**
   - **Definition:** The physical resilience, cell density, and base health scaling. 
   - **Impact:** Determines Max HP.
   - **Flora Base:** Very High.
   - **Bug Base:** Very Low.

2. **Strength (STR)**
   - **Definition:** Muscle density, bite force, and kinetic transfer potential.
   - **Impact:** Determines Physical Damage, carrying capacity, and push force.
   - **Beast Base:** High.

3. **Agility (AGI)**
   - **Definition:** Reaction speed, fast-twitch muscle response, and joint flexibility.
   - **Impact:** Determines Movement Speed, Dodge Chance, and Attack Speed. 
   - **Bug/Bird Base:** Extremely High.

4. **Intelligence (INT)**
   - **Definition:** Synaptic processing power, mana conductivity, and problem-solving capability.
   - **Impact:** Determines Max Mana pool, Spell Damage, and ability to learn complex behaviors.
   - **Sapient Base:** High.

5. **Wisdom (WIS) [New]**
   - **Definition:** Intuition, mana regeneration, and resistance to mental/magical trauma.
   - **Impact:** Mana Regen rate, Magic Resistance, and environmental awareness perimeter.

6. **Luck (LUK) [New]**
   - **Definition:** Karmic alignment and probability manipulation.
   - **Impact:** Critical Hit Chance, Item Drop Rarity (if an entity hoards items), and chance to survive lethal blows at 1 HP.

## Lifespans (Biological Clocks)
Every species is burdened with a maximum natural lifespan. If they hit this limit without evolving or absorbing specific life-extending magic/minerals, they die of old age.

- **Mayfly/Gnats (Base Bug):** 7 to 14 Cycles (Hours).
- **Beetles/Scavengers:** 60 to 120 Cycles.
- **DireWolves/Beasts:** 3,000 to 5,000 Cycles.
- **Sapients (Humans):** 25,000 Cycles.
- **Sapients (Elves):** 100,000 Cycles.
- **Flora (Basic Grass):** 500 Cycles.
- **Flora (Ancient Oak):** 500,000 Cycles.

*Note: Evolving into a UBM (Unique Boss Monster) halts natural aging entirely.*
