# 02 - Type Skills & Species Genetics

## Skill Mechanics
Skills are hardcoded genetic memories. They level up through repeated use.
- **Format:** `[Skill Name] (Type) - Max Level`

### Flora (Plant-Type Skills)
*Plants are stationary, relying entirely on absorption and defense.*
1. **[Photosynthesis] (Passive) - Lvl 10**
   - Grants minimal XP and Energy during daylight cycles.
2. **[Root Network] (Active) - Lvl 5**
   - Drains water/minerals from surrounding nodes. Higher levels increase radius.
3. **[Toxic Spores] (Active/Defense) - Lvl 10**
   - Releases an AoE poison cloud when HP drops below 50%. Scales with INT.

### Bug (Insect-Type Skills)
*Bugs rely on swarming, survival, and scavenging.*
1. **[Chitin Carapace] (Passive) - Lvl 20**
   - Flat reduction to physical damage.
2. **[Scavenger's Greed] (Passive) - Lvl 5**
   - Increases XP gained by eating dead entities by 50%.
3. **[Hive Mind] (Passive/Aura) - Lvl 5**
   - If 3+ bugs of the same species are within 10 meters, all gain +20% AGI and STR.

### Beast (Animal-Type Skills)
*Predators focused on bursts of damage and tracking.*
1. **[Blood Scent] (Passive) - Lvl 10**
   - Increases detection radius of entities under 30% HP.
2. **[Jaw Lock] (Active) - Lvl 15**
   - A heavy physical strike that roots the target in place for 2 seconds. Scales with STR.
3. **[Pack Hunter] (Aura) - Lvl 5**
   - Similar to Hive Mind, but specifically boosts Critical Hit Chance (LUK) when surrounding prey.

### Sapient / Monster (Humanoid/Apex Skills)
*Complex beings capable of tool use and advanced magic.*
1. **[Tool Proficiency] (Passive) - Lvl 50**
   - Allows the equipping of weapons/armor that override base stats.
2. **[Language/Barter] (Passive) - Lvl 10**
   - Allows non-hostile interactions with other Sapients.
3. **[Mana Convergence] (Active) - Lvl 100**
   - Converts INT into physical force, allowing a "Magic Strike".
