# 03 - Special Abilities & Magic Spells

## Special Abilities (Innate Traits)
Unlike Skills (which are leveled up through use), **Special Abilities** are Genetic Absolutes. They are part of the 64-bit Genome and are either On or Off. They only awaken through extreme Trauma or UBM Mutation.

### The 7 Arch-Traits (Dormant in 1% of the population)
1. **[Immortality Clause]**
   - **Effect:** The entity halts natural aging. No longer decays from time. Usually awakens in Ancient Oaks or High Elves.
2. **[Apex Predator]**
   - **Effect:** Deals +300% damage to entities with a lower Level. Fleeing AI (Prey) suffers a 50% Speed penalty when within a 100m radius of an Apex.
3. **[Swarm Sovereign]**
   - **Effect:** Can command up to 10,000 Lesser entities of the same species. (Usually awakens in a Scarab UBM).
4. **[Arcane Conduit]**
   - **Effect:** Base INT acts as a multiplier for all physical attacks. (10 STR * 10 INT = 100 Damage).
5. **[Absolute Carapace]**
   - **Effect:** Negates all physical damage from entities 5 Levels below the user.
6. **[Bioluminescence/Hypnosis]**
   - **Effect:** Pulsing light that overrides the "Fleeing" AI of prey, forcing them to walk towards the user.
7. **[Mana Devourer]**
   - **Effect:** Instead of eating Physical HP to survive, the entity literally eats the INT of the target.

## Magic Spells (The Arcane Matrix)
Magic is the mathematical manipulation of the VRAM's environmental state. It costs Mana (derived from INT). Spells are usually only accessible to Sapients or Arcane UBMs.

### Tier 1 (Common)
- **[Lesser Fireball] (Cost: 10 MP)**: Creates a small physical projectile in the GPU matrix that detonates on impact. Sets the "Burning" trauma flag on the target.
- **[Heal Wound] (Cost: 15 MP)**: Rapidly accelerates cell division, restoring VIT.
- **[Mana Shield] (Cost: 20 MP)**: Creates a temporary buffer equal to 50% of the user's INT that absorbs damage before HP.

### Tier 2 (Advanced)
- **[Earth Spike] (Cost: 50 MP)**: Manipulates the Z-axis of the terrain matrix beneath a target, launching them upwards and dealing massive physical trauma.
- **[Arcane Bolt] (Cost: 40 MP)**: A homing projectile that never misses, scale based entirely on the caster's INT and the target's WIS (Resistance).

### Tier 3 (UBM / World-Tier)
*These spells are catastrophic and usually trigger massive server load. They rewrite the basic rules of the grid.*
- **[Meteor Swarm] (Cost: 5,000 MP)**: Spawns 100 high-velocity projectiles from Z=5000 down to the terrain, wiping out all Flora and low-level Bugs in the radius.
- **[Absolute Zero] (Cost: 10,000 MP)**: Halves the *Speed* variable of every entity within a 10,000-unit radius in VRAM. Represents freezing time/sluggishness.
- **[Resurrection] (Cost: All MP)**: Restores a dead entity's Soul File from the graveyard table back into the active SQLite Vault.
